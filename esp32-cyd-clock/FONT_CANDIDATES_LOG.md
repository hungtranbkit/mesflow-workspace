# Clock Font Candidate Comparison

Evidence: real screenshots captured from the physical panel via the
TFT_eSPI-native `/debug/screenshot` (`tft.readRect()`, see
`screenshot.h`), not a PC mock. All three candidates were generated by
`tools/gen_clock_font.py`, targeting 240-255 x 85-95px for "14:25",
and rendered through the same split HH/colon/MM layout (`display.cpp`).

Note: captured screenshots show light color fringing around text edges
(yellow/blue channel misalignment). This is a `tft.readRect()` SPI
readback artifact from this round's new capture method, not a real
defect in the physical rendering -- the panel draws solid, flat colors
via `fillRect()`/`drawString()`; there is no code path that could
introduce per-channel fringing on the actual display. Shape/spacing/
proportion comparisons below are unaffected (still clearly legible).

## Candidates

| # | Source | Measured (ink, offline) | fontHeight() on device (yAdvance, not ink) |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | LiberationSansNarrow-Bold | 253x93 | 125 |
| B | NimbusSans-Bold | 254x89 | 122 |
| C | DejaVuSansMono-Bold | 255x87 | 136 |

(`fontHeight()`'s larger numbers are TFT_eSPI reporting the GFXfont's
line-spacing metric, not the tight glyph ink height -- expected, not a
bug; see `display.cpp`'s comment.)

- **A (capture-009)**: semi-condensed, natural humanist digit shapes,
  bold without being blocky. Best match for "modern digital clock but
  not too square" -- distinctive without looking generic.
- **B (capture-010)**: regular (non-condensed) width, clean but more
  neutral/generic-looking digit shapes than A.
- **C (capture-011)**: monospace -- perfectly stable digit width by
  construction, but the "1" carries a small serif/flag (typical of
  monospace numeral design to fill the fixed-width cell), a slight
  departure from the clean minimal look the other two have.

## Selected: Candidate A (LiberationSansNarrow-Bold)

Best overall balance of boldness, natural (not squeezed) proportions,
modern-but-not-blocky character, and target-box fit. Set via
`display.cpp`'s `CLOCK_FONT_CANDIDATE 0`. `ClockFontB.h`/`ClockFontC.h`
are kept in the repo (not deleted) in case of a future revisit.
